Kineva — Home screen concepts

Six directions for the Today tab. Each keeps a Liquid Glass material system (translucent slabs, specular top edge, saturated blur) but drops the day-cycle scenic illustration in favour of an abstract, slowly drifting colour field that takes its hue from the wearer's state. All numbers are test data for one wearer on Sunday 6 September.

Scores use the app's own tokens (Charge green, Effort amber, Rest violet, mint accent). Sleep depth is drawn as a single-hue ramp with a legend so it survives colour-vision deficiency. References were pulled from Mobbin and are linked under each concept.

Concept 1

Prism — the evolution of today's page

Keeps the three-ring hero, the energy bar and the live heart-rate card the current page already has, but replaces the scenic day-cycle illustration with a colour field whose hue tracks Charge (green when recovered, amber when strained, violet at night). Rings sit on one glass slab with a one-line synthesis under them, then a 2×2 metric grid with baseline deltas and sparklines.

What changes vs today

  • Synthesis line moves inside the hero, so the score is never shown without its reason.
  • Key metrics become tiles with a baseline delta and a 7-day sparkline instead of a flat list.
  • Background is abstract; the "Kineva" wordmark leaves the canvas and returns to the nav.

Concept 2

Halo — one number, then the story

Charge becomes the single hero: a large halo ring whose glow bleeds into the background field. Effort and Rest demote to glass pills under it. A "Why 94" card lists the four drivers with their baseline deltas, then the day reads as a timeline: last night, this morning's run, and the live now-card.

What changes vs today

  • One score to look at first; the other two are still one tap away and always visible.
  • Drivers are explicit, which the current page only shows after tapping Charge.
  • The timeline replaces "Your cards" as the default lower half; cards live behind Customise.

Concept 3

Stack — three full-width score cards

Each score is its own tall glass card with a glow tinted by its state, ordered by the time of day it matters (Rest first in the morning, Effort first in the evening). Every card carries its one chart: hypnogram for Rest, driver pills for Charge, heart-rate zones for Effort. Live HR and Energy collapse to one strip at the bottom.

What changes vs today

  • No rings on the home page; the number is typographic and the chart does the explaining.
  • Card order reflows by time of day, which the current fixed hero cannot do.
  • Deep-glass (darker) slabs keep text contrast high over the coloured field.

Concept 4

Ledger — dense, numbers-first

For the wearer who wants the data, not the framing. The three scores shrink to a compact ring trio in a glass toolbar so they are visible while scrolling, a short "Read" paragraph explains the day, and the bulk of the page is one glass ledger: every key statistic as a row with its baseline, a seven-day sparkline and a delta. Activities are a short list under it.

What changes vs today

  • Most information per scroll of any concept; nothing is hidden behind a card tap.
  • Ledger rows map one-to-one onto the existing Key Metrics customisation.
  • Glass is lighter here; the background field is calmer so the rows stay legible.

Concept 5

Focus — coach first, numbers second

Leads with a sentence, not a score. The Now card adapts to the hour: recovery and a plan in the morning, a wind-down prompt in the evening, with the single most useful action as a full-width button. Quick actions sit under it. Scores appear as a plain glance list, and one correlation insight closes the page.

What changes vs today

  • Charge, Effort and Rest are read as words first; the number is still there but smaller.
  • Primary action changes through the day (Start session, HRV reading, Bedtime).
  • "One thing" surfaces the correlation engine on the home page instead of in Trends.

Concept 6

Bento — a widget grid you rearrange

The home page becomes a grid of glass tiles in three sizes, like the iOS home screen. Charge is the 1×2 anchor, Effort and Rest are singles beside it, live HR spans the row, and everything below is the wearer's choice. Edit mode reorders and resizes; the existing Key Metrics and Your Cards catalogues become the tile library.

What changes vs today

  • One customisation model instead of three (hero, key metrics, your cards).
  • Every tile is also a home-screen widget, so the two stay visually identical.
  • Densest use of the glass material; the field behind it needs to stay low-contrast.

References

Moonlitt — mixed-size glass tiles on a tinted field Apple Fitness — summary tiles with per-tile charts Oura — tile groups per domain

Shared decisions

  • Background. Three slow-drifting colour blobs under a fine grain, hue keyed to state. It reads as "liquid" without a scene, and it costs one Canvas layer instead of a scenic asset per hour of the day.
  • Glass. One material: 28pt blur, 170% saturation, 1pt hairline at 14% white, specular top edge. A darker "deep" variant is used where long text sits over a bright field.
  • Colour. Score hues are the app's own tokens and are always paired with a label and a number, never colour alone. Sleep depth is a single-hue ramp with hatching for REM, so it survives colour-vision deficiency.
  • Tab bar. Floating glass pill, as in iOS 26, in every concept. The Live tab folds into the heart-rate card's "Full day" link.
